# How to Make a Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich

## Assumptions
- The robot is standing in front of a kitchen counter
- The robot's hands are empty
- A loaf of sliced bread is inside a plastic bread bag on the counter
- The bread bag is closed with a twist tie.
- A jar of peanut butter is on the counter
- A jar of jelly is on the counter.
- A butter knife is on the counter
- A clean plate is on the counter
- Both jars are closed

## Instructions

1. Extend your right hand toward the bread bag.
2. Grab the bread bag with the right hand
3. Extend the left hand toward the twist tie
4. Grip the twist tie with the left hand
5. Rotate the twist tie counterclockwise until it separates from the bread bag
6. Place the twist tie on the counter
7. Release the twist tie from the left hand
8. Release the bread bag from your right hand
9. Grip one side of the bread bag that is open, with the right hand
10. Grip the opposite side of the bread bag opening with your left hand
11. Move both hands away from each other while gripping opposite sides of the bread bag opening
12. Release the bread bag with your right hand
13. Insert the right hand into the bread bag
14. Grab one slice of bread with the right hand
15. Remove the right hand from the bread bag while holding the slice of bread
16. Place the slice of bread flat on the clean plate
17. Release the slice of bread
18. Insert the right hand into the bread bag
19. Grab one slice of bread with the right hand
20. Remove the right hand from the bread bag while holding the slice of bread
21. Place the second slice of bread flat on the clean plate beside the first slice
22. Release the second slice of bread
23. Release the bread bag from the left hand
24. Extend the right hand toward the peanut butter jar
25. Grip the peanut butter jar with the right hand
26. Extend the left hand toward the peanut butter jar lid
27. Grip the peanut butter jar lid with the left hand
28. Rotate the lid counterclockwise until it separates from the jar
29. Place the lid on the counter
30. Release the lid from the left hand
31. Place the peanut butter jar on the counter
32. Release the peanut butter jar from the right hand
33. Extend the right hand toward the butter knife.
34. Firmly grasp the handle of the butter knife with the right hand
35. Move the butter knife toward the open peanut butter jar
36. Insert the blade of the butter knife into the peanut butter
37. Move the blade through the peanut butter until peanut butter covers one side of the blade
38. Lift the butter knife out of the peanut butter jar
39. Move the butter knife toward the first slice of bread
40. Place the peanut butter-covered side of the knife against the top surface of the first slcie of bread
41. Move the knife across the surface of the bread to spread the peanut butter
42. Continue to repeat step 41 until the top surface of the first slice is completely covered with peanut butter
43. Place the butter knife on the clean plate without touching either slice of bread
44. Release the butter knife from the right hand
45. Extend the right hand toward the jelly jar.
46. Grip the jelly jar with the right hand.
47. Extend the left hand toward the jelly jar lid.
48. Grip the jelly jar lid with the left hand.
49. Rotate the lid counterclockwise until it separates from the jelly jar.
50. Place the lid on the counter.
51. Release the lid from the left hand.
52. Place the jelly jar on the counter.
53. Release the jelly jar from the right hand.
54. Extend the right hand toward the butter knife.
55. Grip the handle of the butter knife with the right hand.
56. Move the butter knife toward the open jelly jar.
57. Insert the blade of the butter knife into the jelly.
58. Move the blade through the jelly until jelly covers one side of the blade.
59. Lift the butter knife out of the jelly jar.
60. Move the butter knife toward the second slice of bread.
61. Place the jelly-covered side of the knife against the top surface of the second slice of bread.
62. Move the knife across the surface of the bread to spread the jelly.
63. Continue to repeat step 62 until the top surface of the second slice is completely covered with jelly.
64. Place the butter knife on the plate without touching either slice of bread.
65. Release the butter knife from the right hand.
66. Extend the right hand toward the second slice of bread.
67. Grip the second slice of bread by its edges with the right hand.
68. Lift the second slice of bread off the plate.
69. Rotate the second slice of bread until the jelly-covered surface faces downward.
70. Move the second slice directly above the first slice of bread.
71. Lower the second slice until the jelly-covered surface touches the peanut butter-covered surface of the first slice.
72. Release the second slice of bread from the right hand.

## Done When

The task is complete when the two slices of bread are stacked on the plate with the peanut butter-covered and jelly-covered surfaces of the bread are touching each other
